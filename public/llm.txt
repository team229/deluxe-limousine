# Deluxe Limousine Service

> Deluxe Limousine Service is a luxury limousine, party bus, and executive black car provider based in Stanton, CA, serving Anaheim, Orange County, and 37+ surrounding cities across Southern California (Orange County, Los Angeles, and beyond). Owner-operated since 2010, the company offers stretch limousines, a Hummer H2 stretch, Mercedes Sprinter limos, party buses, and executive SUVs for weddings, quinceañeras, prom, corporate travel, airport transfers, and special events, with transparent all-inclusive pricing and 24/7 availability.
