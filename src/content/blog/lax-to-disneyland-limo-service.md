## How Our LAX to Disneyland Limo Service Works

Every transfer is a private, door-to-door ride in a Deluxe vehicle with a professional chauffeur. From the curb to the resort, this is what the service includes:

- **Terminal meet-and-greet.** Your chauffeur meets you at baggage claim with a name sign, takes the luggage, and walks you straight to the car.
- **Real-time flight tracking.** We track your flight from wheels-up and adjust the pickup automatically. If your landing time changes, we're waiting for the flight, not the schedule.
- **Flat, transparent pricing.** The rate is quoted and confirmed in writing before you book. No surge pricing, no hidden fees, and a delayed landing never changes the number.
- **VIP red carpet arrival.** The arrival is part of the vacation, so every reservation includes red carpet service and chilled sodas and refreshments on board.
- **Drop-off where you need it.** Your Anaheim hotel entrance, the Harbor Boulevard drop-off area, or the Downtown Disney drop-off — whichever fits your plans.
