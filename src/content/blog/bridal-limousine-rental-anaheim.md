## Who Needs Bride and Groom Transportation?

Bride and groom transportation helps most when venues sit far apart. A ceremony in one city and a reception in another adds drive time, and drive time causes delays.

It also helps couples who want to enjoy the day. Neither of you should be navigating or worrying about parking in wedding clothes.

## How Far Ahead Should You Book?

Book 3 to 6 months ahead for peak dates. Deluxe Limousine Service states that availability is limited on popular wedding dates and recommends that window.

Early booking matters because wedding dates cluster on weekends. Reserve your vehicle once your venue and date are final. You will have more choices and less pressure.

## How Do You Build a Wedding Day Transportation Timeline?

Start with fixed times and work backward. Your photographer, venue, and officiant set the anchors.

Follow these steps:

1. List every pickup and drop-off address.
2. Add realistic drive times for your wedding day, not a quiet weekday.
3. Include time for photos and loading a gown.
4. Add a buffer of at least 15 minutes between stops.
5. Share the final schedule with your chauffeur in writing.

A written plan prevents the most common problem, which is a late start that pushes every later stop.

## Do You Need a Wedding Getaway Car?

A wedding getaway car Anaheim couples book is simply the final ride from reception to hotel or airport. Many couples keep the same limo for this send- off. Others book a separate luxury limo Anaheim vehicle just for the exit.

Ask whether your hours cover the full reception. Extra time is easier to add before the day than during it.

## What Should You Check Before You Book?

Quality checks matter more than photos. Use this list to compare any limousine service Anaheim offers.

- Permit: California regulates passenger charter-party carriers through the CPUC. Its [charter-party carrier rules](https://docs.cpuc.ca.gov/Published/Graphics/609.pdf)) require advertisements to include the carrier's TCP number. Ask for it and confirm it with the CPUC.
- Insurance: Ask what coverage applies to passengers.
- All-in pricing: Ask what the quote includes. Deluxe states it provides one up-front price.
- Exact vehicle: Confirm the vehicle and seat count in writing.
- Timing: Confirm the pickup time, wait time, and overtime rate.
- Backup plan: Ask what happens if a vehicle has a problem.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Booking after the venue and date are set, but too late.
- Choosing a vehicle that is too small for gowns and a full party.
- Skipping a written timeline.
- Choosing by lowest price without checking permits.
- Forgetting to book the return trip or send-off.
