# Limousine Service Anaheim: Licensed Limo, Sprinter, and Party Bus Rentals for Weddings, Quinceañeras, Proms, and LAX Transfers

Limousine service in Anaheim is pre-booked, chauffeured transportation in a stretch limo, SUV, Sprinter, or party bus, usually priced by the hour or at a flat airport rate. Deluxe Limousine Service is a locally owned company based in Stanton, next to Anaheim, with an in-house fleet of 11 vehicles. In California, limousine companies must hold a charter-party carrier (TCP) permit from the California Public Utilities Commission.

**How can I tell if an Anaheim limo company is licensed?**

Look for a TCP number. The CPUC says carriers should display their TCP permit number on vehicles and in advertising, and you can confirm a company's status with the CPUC. Deluxe Limousine Service operates under CPUC charter-party carrier (TCP) authority.

**How far ahead should I book a limo in Anaheim?**

For peak wedding dates, book three to six months ahead. Spring weekends fill early because proms and weddings overlap. Airport transfers and smaller events often need less notice, and same-day requests depend on availability.

## What Is Limousine Service in Anaheim?

It is a private charter: your group gets exclusive use of the vehicle and a professional chauffeur for a set time or trip. Unlike rideshare, the chauffeur waits between stops, the price is agreed in advance, and there is no surge pricing.

## Why Choose Professional Limousine Service in Anaheim?

Licensing, insurance, and inspections separate a legal charter from a risky one. Before you pay a deposit, check five things:

1. **TCP permit.** Verify the number with the CPUC.
2. **Vehicle inspections.** The CPUC requests CHP safety inspections for vehicles seating more than 10 people, including the driver, and for modified limousines. Ask when the vehicle was last inspected.
3. **A written contract.** The CPUC recommends getting the price, deposit, cancellation policy, and pickup and drop-off times in writing.
4. **Who actually drives.** Some companies broker bookings to outside operators. We use only our own vehicles and chauffeurs.
5. **Airport permits.** Charter operators serving LAX are licensed through the LAWA Ground Transportation Permit Program.

Deluxe Limousine Service has served Orange County for more than 15 years. The owner answers the phone, and our chauffeurs arrive 10 to 15 minutes before pickup. These are the details we plan around:

- **Travel between venues.** Ceremonies and receptions are often in different cities. Build travel time into the schedule so photos don't eat into it.

- **LAX timing.** We track your flight in real time and do not charge for waits caused by airline delays. For departures, allow extra time for I-5 and I-405 traffic.
