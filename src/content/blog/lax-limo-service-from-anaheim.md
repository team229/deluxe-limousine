**1. How far in advance should I book LAX limo service from Anaheim for an early morning flight?**

At least 24 to 48 hours ahead, generally, especially for early morning departures when availability across the industry tends to be tighter. Same-day booking is sometimes possible depending on the service, but booking ahead guarantees your preferred pickup time and vehicle.

A reputable service tracks the actual flight status, not just the scheduled time on the ticket, so pickup adjusts automatically either way. Worth confirming this specifically when booking, since not every provider offers real flight tracking as standard.

**3. Is it actually cheaper to book a limo for a group instead of separate rideshare cars?**

Often yes, once you factor in that one vehicle can handle a group with luggage that would otherwise need two or three separate rideshares. It also kills the hassle of coordinating multiple pickups and drop-offs at different times.

**4. Can I request a specific vehicle, like an SUV instead of a sedan?**

Most limo services offer a range of vehicle options and let you pick based on group size and luggage when booking. Worth specifying this upfront rather than assuming whatever default vehicle shows up will fit everyone comfortably.

**5. Do limo services charge extra if my luggage takes a while at baggage claim?**

Policies vary, but a lot of companies include a reasonable grace period for baggage claim and customs before any extra waiting fees kick in. Confirm this before booking so there's no surprise if your arrival process runs long.
